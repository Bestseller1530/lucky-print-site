02 / CAPACITY & TURNAROUND

Local production.
Ready for your volume.

Based at 3190 Lenworth Dr, Mississauga, Ontario — in the Toronto area, 15 minutes from Pearson.

Built for fine detail

Epson SureColor P9000 44" with 10-channel UltraChrome HDX pigment inks, a canvas stretching and framing station, and a 44" trimmer.

250+ rolled prints / day

Rolled canvas and paper prints. Up to 100 stretched or framed units per day. Single-item orders, no minimums.

1–2 business days production

Same-day dispatch once production is complete. Canada Post, Purolator and UPS at contract rates. Transit: 1–3 days in Ontario / Quebec, 2–5 days elsewhere in Canada. US shipping via UPS.

Your brand, all the way

Unbranded packaging and merchant inserts on request. Corner protectors and double-wall boxes for framed items; tubes for rolled products.
